Run a gun shop in a neon city.

Cyberpunk Gun Shop Simulator is a shop sim where you buy weapons from megacorporations, stock your shelves, set your prices and serve the customers who walk in off the street. Grow from one small room to a fully expanded shop with staff, a storage room and a firing range in the basement.

THE GAME

MANAGE YOUR SHOP

Design it, stock it, price it

Place shelves, racks and display counters, set your prices and keep the line moving. Price too high or keep people waiting and they leave a bad review.

24 GUNS, 8 MEGACORPS

Climb the license ladder

Start with cheap Ferrocene street guns and work up to Halvorsen-Okada smart rifles, plus ammo, grenades and weapon mods.

FIRING RANGE

Rent out lanes, chase the high score

Build a range under your shop. Customers pay to try your guns, or step up yourself and see how high you can score in ten seconds.

MYSTERY CRATES

Collect rare and gold finishes

Open crates for guns, mods and collectible finishes. Display them, sell them to collectors or quick sell them. In-game credits only.
